For planar SQ devices the zero-order survival bound at 50 M/ml is L* ≈ 126 µm (function/GSIS, p_c = 5 mmHg, is tighter). A 500 µm-full slab (L = 250 µm) is over that bound; Papabathini-class data at L = 250 µm / 50 M/ml reported ~32% viability.

Edmonton-like mass (70 kg, 11k IEQ/kg) is ~1.2e9 cells. At 80 M/ml that is ~15 ml — about 650 cm² of two-sided omental sheet if you respect 0.9 L*_func. Thickening the slab is the degree of freedom that the bound forbids.
