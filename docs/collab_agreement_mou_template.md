## Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MoU") is entered into on this ____ day of ____________, 2027, by and between:

1. **The In Silico Bioengineering Consortium** ("Computational Partner"), developers of the open-source `t1d_simulator` digital twin platform, and
2. **[Partner Institution / Biotech Name]** ("Experimental Partner"), located at [Address / Location].

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope

The purpose of this MoU is to establish a non-binding framework for collaborative research in computational modeling, *in silico* screening, and *in vitro/in vivo* validation of cell replacement therapies for Type 1 Diabetes (T1D).

---

## 2. Roles & Responsibilities

### 2.1. Computational Partner Responsibilities:
- Provide customized computational failure analysis (`t1d_simulator`) tailored to Experimental Partner's specific construct geometry, hydrogel chemistry, cell loading density, and transplantation site.
- Perform parameter sensitivity sweeps and deliver detailed simulation reports outlining predicted oxygen profiles ($pO_2$), FBR fibrotic growth ($L_{fib}$), and 0–48h IBMIR cell retention.

### 2.2. Experimental Partner Responsibilities:
- Provide anonymized physical and biological baseline parameters (e.g., hydrogel diffusion coefficients, cell consumption rates $V_{max}$, construct dimensions).
- Share non-confidential *in vitro* or *in vivo* validation data (viability assays, histology, CGM profiles) to enable iterative calibration of the digital twin engine.

---

## 3. Intellectual Property & Publication Rights

- **Pre-Existing IP**: Each party retains full ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property, background code, materials, and proprietary cell lines.
- **Open-Source Core**: Enhancements to the core public digital twin engine (`t1d_simulator`) remain open-source under the MIT / Academic License.
- **Joint Publications**: Scientific findings resulting from joint computational-experimental studies will be published co-authored in peer-reviewed journals (e.g., *Nature Biotechnology*, *Biomaterials*, *Diabetes*).

---

## 4. Term & Termination

This MoU shall take effect on the date of execution and remain valid for a period of two (2) years. Either party may terminate this MoU upon thirty (30) days' written notice.
